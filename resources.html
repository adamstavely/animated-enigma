Glossary

The acronyms you'll see across this blog.

UXUser Experience
Everything a person perceives and feels while using a product or service.
UIUser Interface
The screens, controls, and feedback through which a person operates a system.
HCDHuman-Centered Design
An iterative approach that grounds every design decision in the needs and context of real users.
HMTHuman-Machine Teaming
People and intelligent systems working interdependently toward a shared goal.
XAIExplainable AI
Methods that make a model's outputs and reasoning understandable to the people who use them.
HCIHuman-Computer Interaction
The research discipline studying how people interact with computing systems.
SASituation Awareness
Perceiving what's happening, understanding what it means, and projecting what comes next.
WCAGWeb Content Accessibility Guidelines
The W3C standard for making web content accessible to people with disabilities.
