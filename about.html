The people behind the [Hu]man Factor

We write about the space where human judgment meets machine capability: how to design it, how to govern it, and how to make sure the people in the loop stay in control.

Why this blog exists

Most writing about AI focuses on the model. Most writing about UX assumes the system behaves predictably. Neither holds once intelligent systems enter high-stakes work.

The Human Factor sits in the gap. We draw on aviation, defense and intelligence systems, enterprise platform design, and academic research on trust and automation to offer practical guidance for teams building technology that people depend on.
